Chimney Sweeping & Cleaning

Why should I clean my chimney?

Safety. A dirty chimney can contribute to fires and severe structural damage.

Health. Damaged or aging liners can contribute to carbon monoxide entering the home.

Efficiency. Soot and creosote buildup can restrict airflow and reduce fireplace performance.

Annual cleaning and inspection can help keep your fireplace operating safely and efficiently.

Chimney & Fireplace Inspection Services

Don’t wait until it’s too late.

Chimney problems are often noticed only after the system stops venting properly.

Level 1 Chimney Inspection. A visual examination of accessible portions of the chimney, including the firebox, damper, flue, flashing, crown, and cap.

Level 2 Chimney Inspection. A more comprehensive examination that may use a camera and specialized tools to inspect concealed areas.

After the inspection, you receive a detailed report of the chimney’s condition and recommended next steps.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How often should the chimney be swept?

The live site recommends professional chimney cleaning and inspection at least once each year. Final published guidance should be reviewed against the current NFPA and CSIA wording.

Why is it important to clean my dryer vent?

Lint buildup can increase fire risk, reduce dryer efficiency, and make drying cycles take longer.

How much does chimney sweeping cost?

Price varies by system condition, chimney size, access, location, and the scope confirmed during booking or inspection.

How often should I clean my dryer vent?

Annual cleaning is a common recommendation, though the best interval depends on use, vent length, and system condition.

What makes a chimney unsafe?

Common concerns include excess creosote, damaged liners, blocked drafting, damaged components, and a missing or failed cap.

Why is air duct cleaning necessary?

Cleaning may help remove accumulated dust and debris when inspection confirms the service is appropriate.

What is the Chimney Star guarantee?

Guarantee language and terms should be confirmed by Chimney Star before this statement is published.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

Duration depends on the home, number of vents, access, system layout, and level of buildup.

Does chimney sweeping make a mess?

Technicians protect the work area and use controlled cleaning methods, then clean the area after the service.

How loud is air duct cleaning?

Professional vacuum and compressed-air equipment can be similar to, or louder than, a household vacuum.

Does a gas fireplace chimney need to be swept?

Gas systems still need regular inspection and maintenance. The exact service depends on the appliance and venting system.

What are signs that my dryer vent needs cleaning?

Long drying times, very hot clothing, unusual odors, and excess lint near the dryer or exterior vent are common warning signs.
