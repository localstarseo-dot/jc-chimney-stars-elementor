These answers are intentionally concise. Service-specific pages can hold the deeper guidance.

Ask a Question
How do I know whether I need a sweep or an inspection?+

An inspection identifies current conditions and helps determine whether cleaning, repair, or another service is appropriate. If the system has not been checked recently, inspection is the safer starting point.

What can cause a chimney leak?+

Water can enter around the cap, crown, flashing, masonry, siding, or nearby roof transitions. The repair should match the confirmed entry point rather than relying on a generic seal.

Will chimney sweeping make a mess?+

Professional sweeping should include protection for the nearby work area, controlled debris removal, and cleanup before the technician leaves.

Can you help with dryer vents and air ducts?+

Yes. Chimney Star’s current service inventory includes dryer vent and air duct services in addition to chimney and fireplace work.
